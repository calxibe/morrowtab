Contact

Get in touch about MorrowTab

MorrowTab is built and published by FixQuotes, and one route reaches the people who work on it: the FixQuotes contact page, with the MorrowTab category already selected for you. That route is the only support channel — there is no forum, no mailing list and no second inbox. Before you write, it is worth reading the troubleshooting section of the manual, because it already covers the problems that come up most.

What to send

Three Kinds of Message

All three go to the same place. The difference is what to put in the message, and how much of it you can answer for yourself first.

🐞

Something is broken

Start with the manual's troubleshooting section. If it does not cover what you are seeing, describe the problem as plainly as you can — these are the details that make it possible to reproduce.

  • Which browser and which version number — MorrowTab needs Chrome or Edge on Chromium 114 or newer
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
  • The exact wording of any message MorrowTab showed you
  • Whether it happens on the new tab page, in the Quick Save popup, or in both
  • Whether it still happens in a fresh new tab — the unlock state is per-tab, so every new tab starts with ordering locked
🔒

A question about your data

The privacy policy lists every storage key MorrowTab writes and every address it can reach. If your question is not answered there, ask — but please leave your own data out of the message.

  • Do not attach an exported backup. It is plain JSON, and it contains every board name, link title and address you have saved
  • Boards travel through your browser's own extension sync, which is not end-to-end encrypted, so treat board names and saved addresses as non-confidential
  • MorrowTab can reach three fixed addresses — fixquotes.com, api.fixquotes.com and images.pexels.com — plus one site you name yourself, if you turn on page-title autofill and grant that site access
💡

An idea for a future version

Ideas are read, and none of them is promised. Describing the job you were trying to do is more useful than naming the control you had in mind, because there is often already a way to do it.

  • Say what you were trying to get done, and where MorrowTab got in the way
  • Check the manual first — per-link "opens in" overrides, usage-based ordering and column export already exist
  • MorrowTab is a Chromium extension, so anything that would need Firefox or Safari is out of scope
  • The 100 KB byte budget comes from browser extension sync; the item ceilings (30 boards, 250 links, 200 per column) are MorrowTab's own, chosen to stay safely inside it
📦

Writing to ask where to install it?

MorrowTab 1.0.0 is on its way to the Chrome Web Store and Microsoft Edge Add-ons. This site will link straight to both listings the day they go live, and installing will then be the ordinary one-click store install. Until then there is nothing to download and nothing to set up.
